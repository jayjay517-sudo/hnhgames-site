Jackpotopia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · H&H Games (HNH CO., LTD.)

The short version. Jackpotopia has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or anything else that identifies you. Your coins and progress stay on your device. If you allow it, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so we can balance it. You can say no, and nothing about the game changes.

1. Your game data stays on your device

Your coins, level, bonuses and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. If your device backs up apps (iCloud or Google backup), this save may be included in that backup under your own Apple or Google account.

2. Gameplay statistics — only if you agree

The first time you open Jackpotopia, we ask whether you are willing to share anonymous gameplay data. Until you say yes, nothing is sent. The app ships with collection switched off, so this is true from the very first second the app runs.

If you agree, the game records these events:

  • spin_batch — a summary of every 25 spins: total bet, total win, average and largest win, coin balance, level and machine
  • coins_earned — free or bought coins received, and from where (time bonus, daily bonus, level-up reward, refill, purchase)
  • balance_zero — your coins ran out: level, number of spins, and minutes since you installed the game
  • store_open, offer_view, offer_tap — the coin store was opened, which offers were shown, and which one you tapped
  • iap_success / iap_fail — a coin purchase completed (product and coins delivered) or did not (cancelled, error, or not available in your region)
  • bet_change, machine_open, level_up — bet size changes, which machine you opened, and level-ups
  • which screen you are on (lobby, machine, store), your level band (for example “5–9”) and whether you have ever made a purchase

Alongside these, Google Analytics for Firebase records a few standard events of its own — the first launch, the start of a session, time spent in the app, and completed in-app purchases (product, price and currency, as reported by the App Store or Google Play) — and a randomly generated app instance identifier, your device model and operating system version, app version, language setting, and a country derived from your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored.

3. Purchases

Coin purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, payment card or billing address. The store tells the app which product was bought and gives it a transaction number; the app uses these only to deliver your coins and to make sure a purchase is not delivered twice. Recent transaction numbers are kept on your device for that purpose.

4. Region check before purchases

Coin purchases are not available in some places (currently the U.S. state of Washington). When you open the coin store and your store account is set to the United States, the app asks our server, run on Cloudflare Workers, for the country and state of your connection. The server works this out from your IP address, returns only the country and state, and does not store anything. The answer is kept on your device for 24 hours. This check runs whether or not you agreed to gameplay statistics, because it is needed to follow the law; it does not run for store accounts outside the United States.

5. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account
  • Your advertising identifier — we switch it off at build time, and the game has no ads
  • Your precise location
  • Contacts, photos, files, or anything else on your device

We do not sell data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

6. Who processes it

Gameplay statistics are processed by Google LLC through Google Analytics for Firebase, acting as our processor (Firebase Privacy and Security). The region check is processed by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Privacy Policy). Both may process data on servers outside the Republic of Korea and outside your own country.

7. How long it is kept

Gameplay event data is deleted automatically after 14 months. Aggregate statistics — counts and averages that describe no individual — may be kept without a time limit.

8. Your choices

You can change your mind at any time: open a machine, tap Info, and switch Share gameplay data off. Collection stops immediately. Declining changes nothing about the game.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the legal basis for gameplay statistics is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw as above; the region check relies on our legal obligations and legitimate interest in complying with local law (Article 6(1)(c) and (f)). You have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we hold no identifier that links data to you as a person, we may not be able to find a specific player's records; switching collection off stops anything further from being sent. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

9. Age

Jackpotopia is a social casino game intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, please contact us and we will delete what we can identify.

10. Changes

If what we collect changes, we will update this page, and if the change is significant we will ask you again in the game.

1. 게임 데이터는 기기에만 저장됩니다

코인, 레벨, 보너스, 설정은 이용자 기기에만 저장되며 저희에게 전송되지 않습니다. 기기가 앱을 백업하는 경우(iCloud 또는 Google 백업) 이 저장 내용이 이용자 본인의 Apple·Google 계정 백업에 포함될 수 있습니다.

2. 플레이 통계 — 동의하신 경우에만

Jackpotopia를 처음 여실 때 익명 플레이 데이터 공유 여부를 여쭙습니다. 동의하시기 전에는 아무것도 전송되지 않습니다. 앱은 수집이 꺼진 상태로 배포되므로 앱이 실행되는 첫 순간부터 이 원칙이 적용됩니다.

동의하시면 다음 이벤트를 기록합니다.

  • spin_batch — 25판마다의 요약: 총 베팅, 총 당첨, 평균·최대 당첨, 코인 잔액, 레벨, 머신
  • coins_earned — 무료로 받거나 구매한 코인과 그 출처(시간 보너스, 일일 보너스, 레벨업 보상, 리필, 구매)
  • balance_zero — 코인이 바닥난 시점: 레벨, 스핀 수, 설치 후 경과 시간(분)
  • store_open, offer_view, offer_tap — 코인 상점을 연 것, 보인 특가, 누른 상품
  • iap_success / iap_fail — 코인 구매 완료(상품, 지급한 코인) 또는 실패(취소, 오류, 지역 제한)
  • bet_change, machine_open, level_up — 베팅 변경, 연 머신, 레벨업
  • 현재 화면(로비, 머신, 상점), 레벨 구간(예: “5–9”), 구매 이력이 있는지 여부

이와 함께 Google Analytics for Firebase가 자체 기본 이벤트(첫 실행, 세션 시작, 앱 사용 시간, App Store·Google Play가 알려주는 인앱 구매 완료 — 상품, 가격, 통화)와 무작위로 만들어진 앱 인스턴스 식별자, 기기 모델·운영체제 버전, 앱 버전, 언어 설정, IP 주소로 추정한 국가를 기록합니다. IP 주소 자체는 저장하지 않습니다.

3. 구매

코인 구매는 전적으로 Apple(App Store) 또는 Google(Google Play)이 처리합니다. 저희는 이름, 결제 카드, 청구지 주소를 볼 수 없습니다. 스토어는 앱에 구매한 상품과 거래 번호만 알려주며, 앱은 이를 코인 지급과 중복 지급 방지에만 씁니다. 이를 위해 최근 거래 번호를 기기에 보관합니다.

코인 구매는 일부 지역(현재 미국 워싱턴주)에서 이용하실 수 없습니다. 코인 상점을 여실 때 스토어 계정 국가가 미국이면, 앱이 Cloudflare Workers에서 운영하는 저희 서버에 접속 위치의 국가와 주를 묻습니다. 서버는 IP 주소로 이를 판단해 국가와 주만 돌려주며 아무것도 저장하지 않습니다. 결과는 기기에 24시간 보관됩니다. 이 확인은 법을 지키기 위해 필요하므로 플레이 통계 동의 여부와 관계없이 이루어지며, 미국 외 스토어 계정에서는 이루어지지 않습니다.
