Privacy Policy

Sudoku Master · H&H Games · Last updated 15 August 2026

한국어로 보기 ↓

Short version: Sudoku Master collects nothing. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network connection of any kind. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not gather, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login and no user profile.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so that it can remember your preferences and your progress between sessions. This data never leaves your phone or tablet.

WhatWhy
Chosen language, colour theme, dark modeSo your settings persist when you reopen the app
Sound and vibration switchesSame reason
Unfinished puzzleSo you can close the app mid-game and continue later
Per-difficulty statistics: games played, best score, average score, average timeTo show your statistics screen

You can erase the statistics at any time from Statistics → Delete Statistics inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything else.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works entirely offline and does not need an internet connection to function. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service and no advertising network in the app.

Third parties

No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected. The app contains no third-party trackers.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: contact@hnhgames.com
